About Private Enterprises

A studio-led interior atelier for India's most exacting brands.

For over 18 years, Private Enterprises has crafted premium interior environments for pharmaceutical majors, corporate offices, hospitals, hotels, educational institutions and discerning residential clients. From concept to keys, we own every millimetre of the build.

Our in-house manufacturing, design and site teams deliver true turnkey execution — modular furniture, cleanroom joinery, glass partitions, false ceiling, aluminium works and MEP — under a single accountable roof.
